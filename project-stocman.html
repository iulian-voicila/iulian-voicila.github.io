Research / STOCMAN

Battery research

STOCMAN

Intelligent management system for improving the efficiency of energy storage systems based on Pb-acid and Li-ion batteries

Project website

Overview

STOCMAN developed an intelligent battery-management approach for lead-acid and lithium-ion storage systems, using fuzzy-logic control of charging and discharging and non-dissipative balancing of the battery voltages to shorten charging time and extend battery life.
