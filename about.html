Measurement, batteries
and teaching.

I am a teaching assistant at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering of POLITEHNICA Bucharest, where I studied power electronics and electric drives and completed a PhD in 2025. My doctoral work, supervised by Prof. George-Călin Serițan, dealt with screening batteries retired from electric vehicles, so that cells with useful capacity left can be identified quickly and reused.

I have taught laboratories in electrical measurements and measurement systems since 2021 and co-authored three laboratory books for them. Since 2026 I have also taught the lectures and laboratories of Data Transmissions and Protocols and of Measurements and Instrumentation. I also write software for teaching, such as the MEE learning app, and publish measurement data openly.

Before joining the university, I worked for three years as a functional safety engineer at Expleo on automotive embedded systems, with ISO 26262 safety analyses and in-vehicle CAN networks.
