Overview

Both datasets come from measurements made at POLITEHNICA Bucharest and are published on IEEE DataPort under CC BY 4.0, with a GitHub mirror in CSV and XLSX form that can be browsed directly.

The first contains 24-hour open-circuit-voltage relaxation records for eight cells across six chemistries (LiFePO4, NMC, LiCoO2, LiPo, NiCd, NiMH) after charge- and discharge-based state-of-charge conditioning, sampled once per minute (30 relaxation traces). It is intended for state-of-charge estimation, OCV and hysteresis analysis and model validation.

The second contains charge and discharge voltage curves for ten LiFePO4 cells under CC-CV charging and three constant-current discharge profiles, 60 curves in total sampled once per second, for capacity estimation and cell-to-cell consistency analysis.
